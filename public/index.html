How the radar works

  • Data. Every candle comes from your Market-Data GitHub repo, the shared store of all your dashboards: Binance spot USDT pairs (1H, 4H, 1D) for the coins, the TradingView gauges uploaded from your PC (1H, 4H) for TOTAL2ES, STABLE.C, USDT.D and USDC.D. Closed candles only: daily bars are Market-Data's closed days (the gauges' days are built from their 4H candles once the 20:00 UTC candle has closed), 8H bars are pairs of 4H candles at 00, 08 and 16 UTC. No TradingView calls. GitHub rebuilds the radar on its own whenever Market-Data gets new candles; Refresh asks GitHub to rebuild now (about a minute), and the strip under the title shows how old each series is.
  • 4H and 1H early warnings. The same test as the 8H tier on a closed 4H or 1H candle, against the same 1D levels: through the level by at least a quarter of that timeframe's ATR, on 1.5× volume, RSI on the breakout side of 50, no divergence, held at Weak while stablecoin dominance breaks up. Fresh = the last three closed candles (12 hours on 4H, 3 hours on 1H). They are scored for every coin. 1H is the earliest and noisiest heads-up; 4H is where entries are refined.
  • 8H early warning. A closed 8H candle (11am, 7pm and 3am Sydney) beyond the coin's 1D trigger level by at least a quarter of the 8H ATR, on volume at least 1.5× its 20-candle average, with 8H RSI on the breakout side of 50 and no RSI divergence into the break. Alt breakouts are held at Weak while stablecoin dominance (USDT.D + USDC.D) is breaking up. A break that closes back inside within two 8H candles is marked Failed. Only the last 24 hours count as fresh.
  • Stages. Early (8H) is a heads-up; Confirmed is the 1D close through the level with daily RSI on the same side of 50; the 1W trend (weekly RSI above or below 50) shows whether the move runs with or against the bigger picture.
  • Levels. When a triangle, wedge or flag is found, resistance and support are its trendlines projected to today; otherwise the nearest swing high above and swing low below the price. Weekly bars are built from the daily bars.
  • Scores (0–100). Price near the level, a bullish or bearish pattern, a Bollinger squeeze, trend (EMA20/50/200), RSI, MACD histogram, volume, RSI divergence and strength vs BTC over 30 days. 60% daily, 40% weekly. Watch means 55+ and at least 15 points ahead of the other side. Coiling means a squeeze in the tightest 20% with no side ahead. Broke out / down means a daily close through the level in the last three closed bars that still holds.
  • Chance and days. Every past daily close through a swing level in each coin's history is replayed: stop 1.5× ATR back through the level, target 3× ATR beyond it (2:1), 45 days allowed. Chance is how often the target came first, blended with the all-coin rate (weight of 10 cases). Days is the middle half of how long the winners took. A 2:1 trade breaks even at 33%.
  • Before acting, wait for the daily close (11am Sydney), check volume, and prefer the retest. This is a screen, not financial advice.
